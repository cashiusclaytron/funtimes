The Advisor

Reformed lawyer. Retired dad-of-the-year. Permanent ally.

I started my career in law, where I learned that the most important part of any deal is the part nobody wants to read. Then I traded billable hours for bedtime stories and spent eight years as a stay-at-home dad — running budgets, managing chaos, and negotiating with people who do not accept "because I said so" as a closing argument.

Real estate, it turns out, is the two jobs combined. I read the contracts like the lawyer I was and I protect your home like the dad I still am. No high-pressure tactics, no "trust me" hand-waving — just a steady ally who's genuinely on your side. Think of me as the Obi-Wan of escrow: calm, a little gray in the beard, and very hard to trick.
